✦ FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Do you only edit English language videos?

Yes — I only edit English language videos. This ensures the highest quality in caption accuracy, pacing and communication throughout the editing process.

How many revisions do I get?

Every project includes 2 free revisions. And if you need any tiny change after that — no problem at all, just ask. I want you to be completely happy with the result.

How do I send you my raw footage?

You can send files via Google Drive, WeTransfer or Dropbox — whichever is easiest. I'll share a folder link once we start working together.

How long does editing take?

Reels and short-form: 24–48 hours. Talking head videos: 1–2 days. Podcasts and long-form: 2–3 days. Rush delivery is also available.

Do you add captions and subtitles?

Yes! Animated captions are one of my specialties — especially for Talking Head, Podcast and Reels where people watch without sound.

Can you edit in different aspect ratios?

Yes — 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1 for Instagram feed. Just tell me what format you need.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes! I work with clients worldwide and adapt to your time zone. All communication and editing is in English.

How do we get started?

Simple — message me on WhatsApp or send an email. Tell me what type of video you need, share your footage, and we'll take it from there. No complicated process!
