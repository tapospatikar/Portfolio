✦ FAQ

Questions? Answered.

How many revisions do I get?

Every project includes 2 free revisions per video. And if you need any tiny change after that — no problem at all, just ask. I want you to be completely happy with the result.

How do I send you my raw footage?

You can send files via Google Drive, WeTransfer or Dropbox — whichever is easiest. I'll share a folder link once we start working together.

How long does editing take?

Short-form content: 24–48 hours. Talking head videos: 1–2 days. Podcasts and ad videos: 2–3 days. Rush delivery is also available if you need it faster.

Do you show your prices?

I don't list fixed prices because every project is different. Reach out on WhatsApp or email, tell me what you need, and I'll give you a custom quote based on your project.

How do we get started?

Simple — message me on WhatsApp or send an email. Tell me what type of video you need, share your footage and references, and we'll take it from there!
